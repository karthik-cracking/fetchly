# Fetchly Browser Extension Privacy Policy

**Fetchly** does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or download history to any remote server.

## How Data Is Used
- All communication happens **locally on your device** between the Fetchly browser extension and the Fetchly desktop application via Chrome/Firefox Native Messaging.
- Permissions (`downloads`, `nativeMessaging`, `cookies`, `storage`, `contextMenus`, `scripting`, `webRequest`, `webNavigation`, `alarms`, and host permissions) are used solely to:
  - Hand off download links and required session cookies/headers directly to your local Fetchly desktop app.
  - Detect video/audio streams on web pages when you choose to download them.
  - Persist your extension preferences locally via browser storage.

No analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking are included.

For questions or issues, visit: https://github.com/karthik-cracking/fetchly
